THE IDEA

How do the methods differ?

No relief

US tax is calculated on the full fee. Angolan tax does not reduce US tax.

Tax deduction

Deduct Angolan tax from the fee before calculating US tax.

Foreign tax credit

Calculate US tax, then offset it with Angolan tax up to the credit limit.

The zero credit limit in Jordan’s case assumes no other foreign-source income in the same category and no treaty re-sourcing. This comparison follows the textbook’s simplified example.
